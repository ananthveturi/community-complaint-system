Differentiation

Designed around what most portals miss

Existing civic platforms already cover reporting, routing, and tracking. The Community Complaint Management System adds a stronger accountability layer: impact-aware intake, transparent service stages, and proof-based closure.

Impact-first intake

Complaint forms guide citizens to explain location, urgency, public risk, and affected people so admins can triage better.

Trust ledger

Every status change, official note, citizen comment, and resolution proof stays visible in one unified timeline.

Closure evidence

A case cannot be marked resolved unless the administrator uploads a definitive proof image of the completed work.

Service learning

Dashboard analytics show categories, status volumes, resolution time, and citizen satisfaction metrics to improve operations.
